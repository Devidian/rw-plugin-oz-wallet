# OZ Wallet 0.11.0

- Configure the initial world treasury and annual funding separately (defaults: 100,000 and 25,000).
- View faction balances and, with the right faction role, their transaction history; administrators can inspect faction accounts.
- Reverse eligible system-account transactions from account details with confirmation. Account tables and page numbers are corrected.

Requires OZ Tools 0.26.3 or newer. Faction views require OZ Factions.
